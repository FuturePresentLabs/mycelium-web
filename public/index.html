Questions, answered

What you probably want to know.

If something is missing, ask us. We would rather answer directly than make the site longer.

Is Mycelium really open source?

Yes. The core is licensed under MIT OR Apache-2.0. You can read it, run it, change it, and self-host it without a license key. The hosted service is a separate convenience layer.

Do I have to bring my own model tokens?

No. You can connect your own provider keys, or buy credits from us. The same choice applies to hardware: bring your own server, or run on ours.

What happens to my data if I leave?

You can export your workspace and take it with you. Hosted workspaces are deleted on a defined schedule after cancellation, with an export window. Self-hosted installs are yours from day one.

Is this another chat app?

No. Chat is one interface. The product is the workspace: agents, shared apps, files, approvals, and the operating context that connects them.

Can I use my existing MCP servers?

Yes. Mycelium is designed to connect to MCP servers alongside the Composio catalog. If your tool exposes MCP, it can be part of the workspace.

Do you train on my data?

No. Your workspace data is not used to train models. Enterprise customers can choose a private deployment and a stricter data boundary.
