Security & trust

Open by default. Yours by design.

You should not have to trust a marketing page with your business. Read the source, run it yourself, scope the access, and leave with your data whenever you want.

Source

Inspect it.

The core is open source under MIT OR Apache-2.0. The build, the access model, and the update path are visible. Security through obscurity is not a strategy.

github.com/mycelium-linux/mycelium →

Data

Keep it.

Your workspace data is yours. Export it. Delete it. Self-host it. We do not train models on customer workspace data, and enterprise deployments can keep the data boundary entirely inside your infrastructure.

Access

Scope it.

Passkeys for people, scoped credentials for agents, explicit approvals for mutations, and an audit trail for the actions that matter. Credentials are leased at the moment of use, not baked into configuration.

Hosting

Isolate it.

Hosted workspaces run on FPL Cloud with per-tenant isolation and encrypted storage. Enterprise customers can choose a dedicated deployment with a private network boundary.
