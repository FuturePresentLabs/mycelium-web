FAQ

Before you buy.

The short version: the open source project is free; the hosted plan is for teams that want someone else to own the pager.

Do I need a credit card for the trial?

No card for the 7-day hosted trial. We ask for payment details when you choose a plan. Self-hosting never requires payment.

Can I move from hosted to self-hosted?

Yes. Export your workspace and follow the self-hosting guide. The open-source install does not require a license key.

What counts as an agent?

An agent is a persistent worker with a job, memory, permissions, and a place in the workspace. You can create, pause, and delete them at any time.

What happens when I hit my limit?

We warn you before the cap. You can upgrade, add a pack, or pause new agent runs. We do not want to surprise you with an overage bill.

Is there an enterprise SLA?

Yes. Enterprise includes a 99.95% uptime SLA, a named support path, private deployment options, and security review support.
