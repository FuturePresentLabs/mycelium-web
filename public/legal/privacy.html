Privacy.

A plain-language summary of what the Mycelium website and hosted service collect, why, and how to get your data out.

What this policy covers

This policy covers the Mycelium website at mycelium.fpl.dev and the hosted Mycelium service operated by Future Present Labs LLC. Self-hosted installations are operated by you; your own policies apply.

What we collect

Account details (name, email, organization), authentication events, billing records, support messages, and product usage needed to run the service. We do not train models on customer workspace data.

What we do not do

We do not sell personal data. We do not use workspace content for advertising. We do not claim a certification we have not earned.

Processors

We use infrastructure and payment processors to operate the service, including Cloudflare and Stripe. Enterprise customers can review the current subprocessor list under a DPA.

Your choices

You can request access, correction, export, or deletion by emailing privacy@fpl.dev. Hosted workspaces have an export window after cancellation.

Future Present Labs LLC · privacy@fpl.dev
